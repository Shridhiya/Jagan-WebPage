When should I get wheel alignment done?

Every 5,000 to 7,000 km, or immediately if you notice the steering pulling to one side, off-center steering wheel, or uneven tread wear on tyre edges.

Yes, every new tyre purchased at Sree Laxmi Automobiles is guaranteed genuine, carrying standard manufacturer warranty codes.
